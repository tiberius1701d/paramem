
## Speaker anchor

- **This session's own speaker anchor is `{speaker_id}`.** If the speaker names THEMSELF ("I'm Alex"), map that name directly onto the anchor — `"Alex": "{speaker_id}"` — instead of a fresh `Person_N` (the one exception to the placeholder shape rule).
