
## Speaker anchor

- `{speaker_id}` is this session's own speaker anchor. ONLY a self-introduction spoken by the `[user]` in the transcript — "I'm Priya", "my name is Priya", "call me Priya" — folds that name onto `{speaker_id}` instead of a fresh `Person_N` (the one exception to the placeholder shape rule), and every coreferring self-naming variant ("Priya", "Priya Sharma") folds onto the SAME anchor. Without such a self-introduction in the transcript, every name in the transcript is a DIFFERENT person and gets an ordinary `Person_N` — no matter what any fact claims.
